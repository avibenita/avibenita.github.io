Three chart modes, one mapping

Assign columns once, then switch between bubble, quadrant, and 3D scatter without rebuilding the chart.

Bubble

Position points by X and Y, encode a third variable as bubble size, and optionally colour and label each case.

  • Size by area or width to match Excel conventions
  • Colour by group or a numeric scale
  • Drag labels beside their bubbles

Quadrant

Split the plane into four regions so high/low combinations on two axes become immediately readable.

  • Reference lines you can move
  • Same X, Y, size, colour, and labels as bubble mode
  • Useful for prioritisation and 2×2 positioning

3D scatter

Add a Z axis for true three-dimensional positioning when a third numeric coordinate matters.

  • Rotate and inspect the cloud of points
  • Keep colour and labels from the same mapping
  • Switch back to bubble or quadrant without remapping
