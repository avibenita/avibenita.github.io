Privacy Policy

How we handle your information across the Statistico™ Platform and Office.js add-in

This Privacy Policy explains what information Statistico™ collects, how it is used, and the choices you have when using the Office Add-in and related web services.

Effective date: July 29, 2026

1. Who We Are

Statistico™ is provided by Metrics Institute ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy applies to the Statistico™ Office Add-in for Excel, the Statistico™ website, and related web services (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Worksheet data you choose to analyze: the specific ranges or datasets you select inside Excel in order to run a statistical procedure. This data is processed to generate the requested results.
  • Account and licensing information: if you register for an account or activate a subscription, we collect your name, email address, and license/subscription status.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: add-in version, feature usage, and error/crash information used to maintain and improve the Service.
  • Microsoft 365 context: limited information provided by the Office.js runtime (such as host application and locale) required for the add-in to function inside Excel.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: used on our website for essential functionality and basic, privacy-respecting analytics.

3. How We Use Information

  • To run the statistical analyses and features you request.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To manage licenses, subscriptions, and entitlements for premium features.
  • To provide support and respond to inquiries.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. Worksheet data you analyze is used solely to produce the results you requested and is not used to train third-party AI models without your consent.

4. AI-Assisted Features

Some features may use AI-assisted processing to help interpret or summarize statistical output. Where this involves sending data to a third-party AI provider, only the minimum data necessary to generate the requested interpretation is transmitted, and it is not retained by us beyond what is required to return the result to you.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Worksheet data processed for an analysis is not permanently stored unless you explicitly save or export results.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

  • With service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g., hosting, payment processing, analytics), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Microsoft, where required for distribution and licensing through Microsoft AppSource and Microsoft 365.
  • When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to continued protection under this Policy.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including under GDPR and similar regulations), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
