# Statistico video review standards

These standards are sent with every automatic review. The reviewer has no access to earlier conversations.

- Preserve Statistico's navy and cyan visual family, logo, typography, restrained camera movement, and shared components.
- Show the control, perform the real action, wait for the current results or charts, then hold the result for about 2–3 seconds where a viewer needs to read it. Avoid blank frames, loading footage, stale results, and cropped labels.
- Demonstrate real interactivity and its analytical consequence. Do not animate invented statistical values or imply a feature that the module does not perform.
- Keep narration explanatory and aligned with the action, and leave enough time to read the result.
- Keep narration stems voice-only and scene-sized. Preserve intentional silence. Keep optional music separate from narration.
- For Independent Means, keep the two silent capability cards (25 seconds and 19 seconds) and the established closing. A silence detector must not treat those cards as failed speech.
- Advertise only verified working capabilities. The capability cards have to match the current module.
- Independent Means delivery is 1728×1080, 30 fps, H.264, with fast-start. Do not treat 1920×1080 as a global requirement.
- Narration loudness stays about −16 to −17 LUFS, with true peak at or below −1 dBTP. Judge intentional silence separately. Do not boost audio that already meets this.
- Automated readiness is not publication approval. Avi views and listens to the candidate, then approves any CapCut export. The workflow must not write the approved folder or replace website assets.

Sampling is not a complete motion review. A transcript does not prove that the voice sounds natural, that pronunciation is correct, that music or noise is absent, or that transitions feel pleasant. Those points stay on the human pass unless a later check is actually implemented and named in the report.
