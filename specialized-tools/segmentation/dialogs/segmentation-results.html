    How the index is calculated

    • Splitting construct.
    • Cut-off.
    • Above average + 1 SD.
    • Factor score. Each dimension you compare is averaged in the same way, for the respondents who answered it.
    • Raw distance. The raw Split Index is the absolute difference of the two group means.
    • Standardized distance. When dimensions do not share a scale, the standardized Split Index divides that difference by the pooled standard deviation. A one-point gap on a 1–5 scale is then not treated as equal to a one-point gap on a 0–100 scale.

    Example

    |4.26 − 3.53| = 0.73
