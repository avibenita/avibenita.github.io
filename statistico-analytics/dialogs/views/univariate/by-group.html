Similarity Profile™

How to read Similarity Profile™ in Grouped Analysis.

This is a practical, non-academic approach original to Statistico. It is not a published coefficient, not a p-value, and not a substitute for a significance test. Use it to describe how alike group distributions look in applied work.

What it is for

After you split a numeric variable by a grouping column, Similarity Profile™ answers a practical question: which groups look alike, and which stand apart? It compares every pair on location, spread, and shape, then rates them from 0 to 100.

The three components

Location — are the groups centered at similar values?
Spread — do they vary by similar amounts?
Shape — after lining up mean and SD, do the density curves overlap?
Overall combines the three with a geometric mean, so one weak component is not hidden by two strong ones.

How to read the scores

90–100 Very similar
75–89 Mostly similar
50–74 Mixed similarity
Below 50 Substantially different
Click a table row to see why a pair scored that way.

What it is not

It does not test whether a difference is statistically significant. For that, use a group comparison procedure (t-test, ANOVA, and related tests). Treat these scores as an effect-size style description of practical similarity, designed here for Statistico.
